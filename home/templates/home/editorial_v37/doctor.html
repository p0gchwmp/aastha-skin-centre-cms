03 / Treatment principles

Assessment before procedure selection.

Similar-looking skin, hair and nail concerns can have different causes. The plan should explain diagnosis, options, limitations and aftercare before treatment begins.

01History first

Symptoms, duration, previous treatment and medical context matter.

02Examine the pattern

Clinical examination guides the likely diagnosis.

03Explain options

Benefits, limitations, downtime and aftercare should be clear.

04Review response

Follow-up allows treatment to be continued, adjusted or simplified.
