# Aastha Skin Centre SEO growth plan

## Goal

Grow qualified organic visibility for dermatologist, skin, hair, laser and procedure searches in Jammu while maintaining medical accuracy, patient usefulness and Indian medical-advertising compliance.

Ranking is not guaranteed. Work is prioritised around discoverability, relevance, trust, local prominence and conversion quality.

## Competitor lessons

### Independent clinic model

High-performing clinic sites build dense but navigable condition and treatment clusters, answer cost/safety/session questions, show clinician authority, publish regularly and link every page to related services and booking paths.

We will not copy exaggerated claims, duplicate near-identical landing pages or search-engine-first wording.

### Directory model

Large directories rank through domain authority, city-specialty pages, locality/service combinations, reviews, profile completeness and extensive internal linking. A clinic website cannot recreate their scale, but can outperform them for high-intent local queries with better first-party expertise, precise clinic information and stronger conversion pages.

## Guardrails

- No guarantee of top rankings.
- No fabricated reviews, awards, patient counts or success rates.
- No unsupported “best”, “No. 1” or guaranteed-result claims.
- No thin doorway pages for every locality.
- No mass-produced pages that merely swap place names.
- Medical content must be genuinely useful and medically reviewed before publication.
