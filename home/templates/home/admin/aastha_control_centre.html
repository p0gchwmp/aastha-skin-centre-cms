Safety rule

No candidate URL, source snapshot or populated field unlocks local schema or locality indexing by itself. Approval requires a verification date, reviewer and verification notes; the proof URL is optional. Conflicting public hours must be resolved against the clinic's current operating schedule before approval. Area pages remain noindex until the verified-only activation step is explicitly applied.
