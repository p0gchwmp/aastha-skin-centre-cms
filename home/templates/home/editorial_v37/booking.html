03 / Before you book

Common appointment questions.

Is the appointment confirmed immediately?
No. The form prepares a WhatsApp request for reception. The clinic confirms the final branch, date and time after receiving it.
Are walk-in patients accepted?
Yes. Walk-ins are accepted, although patients with confirmed appointments receive priority.
Can I request a same-day appointment?
Yes, but same-day availability depends on the clinic schedule and must be confirmed by reception.
Can I book a procedure directly?
Procedure suitability and preparation are usually confirmed after assessment. Contact reception before travelling for a scheduled procedure.
