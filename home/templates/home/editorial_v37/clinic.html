02 / First visit

Bring context, not just the latest photo.

A useful consultation is easier when the history of the concern is clear.

  • Current medicines and supplements
  • Previous prescriptions or procedure details
  • Relevant test reports
  • Skincare or haircare products in use
  • Photos showing how the concern changed
  • Known allergies or past reactions
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding information when relevant
  • Your main treatment goal
  • Questions about downtime or aftercare
