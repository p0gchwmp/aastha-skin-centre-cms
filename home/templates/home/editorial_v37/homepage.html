04 / How care works

The treatment should follow the diagnosis.

Care moves from history and examination to explanation, planning and review.

01 / Understand

Start with what has actually been happening.

Symptoms, duration, previous medicines, skincare, medical history and relevant triggers are reviewed before treatment is chosen.

02 / Examine

Similar-looking concerns can have different causes.

Clinical examination helps establish the likely condition. Investigations may be considered when they could change diagnosis or management.

03 / Explain

Understand the options before agreeing to one.

The likely diagnosis, alternatives, limitations, expected course and relevant aftercare are discussed in clear language.

04 / Plan

One concern does not mean one identical package.

Skin or hair type, medical history, priorities, tolerability and acceptable downtime all influence treatment planning.

05 / Review

Good care changes when the response changes.

When follow-up is needed, progress and tolerability are reassessed so treatment can be continued, adjusted or simplified.
