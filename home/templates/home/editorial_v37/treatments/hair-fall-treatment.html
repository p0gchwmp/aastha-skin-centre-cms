03 / Causes

The cause list is broad for a reason.

Pattern hair loss

Male and female androgenetic patterns can progress gradually and may benefit from earlier treatment aimed at preserving existing follicles.

Telogen shedding

Illness, surgery, childbirth, stress and other physiological changes can trigger diffuse shedding.

Autoimmune

Alopecia areata can cause smooth patches and may involve nails or more extensive hair loss.

Nutritional & hormonal

Iron deficiency, thyroid or hormonal conditions and nutritional factors may contribute in selected patients.

Scalp disease

Dandruff, psoriasis, infection and inflammatory scalp disease can contribute to shedding or discomfort.

Hair practices

Tight hairstyles, heat, chemical damage and traction can injure hair or follicles.

04 / Treatment map

Treat the diagnosis, then choose the tools.

Medical

Prescription treatment

Topical or oral medicines may be selected depending on diagnosis, general health and potential side effects.

Underlying cause

Correct what can be corrected.

Confirmed deficiency, scalp inflammation, infection or another underlying condition may need direct treatment.

Supportive

PRP & GFC

Supportive procedural treatment may be considered in selected cases as part of a wider medical plan.

Restoration

Hair transplant assessment

Surgical restoration is a separate suitability decision and does not replace diagnosis of active hair loss.

Scalp

Treat inflammation

Dandruff, psoriasis, infection or another scalp condition may need direct treatment when present.

Review

Track response

Follow-up helps distinguish expected early shedding, inadequate response, side effects and changing diagnosis.

05 / When to come in

Some hair-loss patterns should not be watched indefinitely.

What signs deserve earlier assessment?
Sudden heavy shedding, bald patches, scalp pain or burning, significant redness or scaling, pustules, eyebrow or eyelash loss, rapidly changing density or signs of scarring deserve dermatology assessment.
Do supplements work for everyone?
No. The cause should be identified and confirmed deficiencies corrected rather than automatically starting supplements for every form of hair loss.
Does every patient need PRP or GFC?
No. Procedures are supportive options in selected cases and should follow diagnosis rather than replace it.
