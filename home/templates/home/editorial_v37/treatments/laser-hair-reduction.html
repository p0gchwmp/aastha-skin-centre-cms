01 / Suitability

Hair colour, thickness and skin tone all matter.

Laser is not equally effective for every hair type, and deeper skin tones require careful device, wavelength, energy and cooling selection.

Dark, coarse hair

Dark, coarse hair often absorbs laser energy more effectively because there is more pigment available for targeting.

05 / Safety & expectations

Good laser work is mostly about judgement.

Can laser hair reduction be done on darker skin?
Many deeper skin tones can be treated when the device, wavelength, settings and cooling are selected carefully. Poorly chosen settings can increase the risk of burns or pigment changes.
Why call it reduction instead of permanent removal?
Because not every follicle responds equally, hair grows in cycles, hormonal factors can activate new growth and maintenance may be needed. The realistic endpoint is long-term reduction in regrowing hair.
Does facial hair need different thinking?
Often yes. Facial hair can be influenced by hormones, especially when there are symptoms such as irregular cycles, acne or scalp thinning. That context affects expectations and maintenance.
